# Life OS — Product Guide

> **One calm system for your whole life.**
> Plan · Track · Reflect

---

## 1. What is Life OS?

Life OS is a personal productivity and life-management application. It brings together
the things people normally scatter across several apps — your to-do list, weekly
schedule, calendar, goals, habits, notes, and a daily journal — into **one connected
system** so that your progress is visible and your planning is simple.

The core idea is that these tools should talk to each other. A task you complete
counts toward a goal. A habit you keep shows up as a streak. A time block in your
weekly timetable can point back to the task it belongs to. Because everything is
connected, you always see how your daily actions add up to your bigger aims.

Life OS is designed to be **self-hosted** — you run it yourself, and **your data stays
yours**.

---

## 2. Who is it for?

Life OS is built for anyone who wants more structure and clarity in their day-to-day
life, for example:

- **Busy professionals** juggling work and personal responsibilities.
- **Students** balancing study, routines, and long-term goals.
- **People building habits** (fitness, learning, mindfulness) who want to see streaks.
- **Anyone** who wants one place to plan the week, track commitments, and reflect.

It suits people who like a clean, focused interface rather than a complex, account-heavy
productivity ecosystem.

## 3. The big picture — how the pieces fit together

| Area | Plain-language purpose |
|------|------------------------|
| **Tasks** | The things you need to do — one-off or repeating. |
| **Projects** | Groups of related tasks with progress and milestones. |
| **Goals** | The outcomes you're working toward, measured in several ways. |
| **Habits** | The recurring behaviours you want to build, tracked with streaks. |
| **Timetable** | Your ideal recurring week — time blocks across Monday–Sunday. |
| **Calendar** | Dated events and scheduled tasks in month / week / day / agenda views. |
| **Notes** | Ideas and reference material, linked to your life areas and projects. |
| **Journal** | A daily reflection with mood, gratitude, and private entries. |
| **Rewards** | Points earned for completing tasks, habits, and milestones. |
| **Life Areas** | Your own categories (Career, Health, Family, …) that colour everything. |
| **Settings** | Your profile, preferences, weekly schedule, and notifications. |

**Life Areas** are the connective tissue: a task, goal, habit, note, project, event, or
timetable block can all belong to a life area, giving you a consistent colour and
category across the whole app.

---

## 4. Getting started

1. **Create your account** from the landing page (**Get started**). You'll provide your
   name, email, and a password.
2. **Complete onboarding** — a quick one-screen setup that captures your name, timezone,
   which day your week starts on, and whether you prefer a 12-hour or 24-hour clock.
3. **Start exploring.** Life OS automatically sets you up with sensible defaults so the
   app isn't empty on day one:
   - Seven starter **Life Areas**: Career, Health, Family, Faith, Finance, Learning,
     Personal — each with its own colour and icon. You can rename, recolour, reorder,
     archive, or add your own at any time.
   - Default preferences and schedule settings you can fine-tune later.
4. Use the **sidebar** to move between areas, or press **⌘K / Ctrl+K** to open the
   **command palette** and jump to any page by typing.

## 5. Feature walkthrough

### 5.1 Tasks

Tasks are the heart of daily execution.

- **Capture a task** with a title, optional notes, priority (Low / Medium / High /
  Urgent), and status (To do / In progress / Completed / Cancelled).
- **Schedule it** with a *do date* (when you plan to work on it) and/or a *due date*,
  plus optional start/end time and an estimated duration.
- **Organise it** by life area, project, parent task (to create subtasks), and tags.
- **Complete it** with one tap on the row's circular checkbox.
- **Repeating tasks:** turn on "repeats" to schedule a task daily, weekly, monthly, or
  yearly — with options like every 2 weeks on chosen weekdays, the 2nd Tuesday of each
  month, or the last Friday of the month. You can end a series on a date or after a
  number of times. For each occurrence you can **skip this time** or **remove it from
  the series** without deleting the whole schedule.
- **Views:** switch between **All**, **Today**, **Upcoming**, **Overdue**, and
  **Completed**. Subtasks show their own mini progress (e.g. "2/5 subtasks").

### 5.2 Projects

Projects group related tasks toward a shared outcome.

- Give a project a name, description, status (Planning / Active / On hold / Completed /
  Archived), priority, start and target dates, and a life area.
- **Track progress** with a progress bar based on completed tasks, plus **milestones**
  you can add, complete, and remove.
- Once finished, **archive** a project to keep it out of the way, and **restore** it if
  needed.
- The project detail shows its tasks at a glance, with a shortcut to the Tasks page.

### 5.3 Goals

Goals capture the outcomes you're working toward, and Life OS lets you measure them in
the way that fits:

- **By milestones** — a simple checklist of steps.
- **By linked tasks** — progress follows the tasks you connect to the goal.
- **By a number** — track a current value against a target (e.g. 12 of 20 books).
- **By sub-goals** — a big goal can contain smaller goals, and progress rolls up.

Goals have a timeframe (Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Yearly), a status, a priority, and
optional links to a life area and a project. You can nest goals under a parent goal.
Marking a goal complete sets it to 100%.

### 5.4 Habits

Habits help you build consistency.

- Create a habit with a frequency — **Every day**, **Weekly target**, or **Specific
  days** — and a target per period.
- **Check in** with one tap for today. Cards show your **current streak**, your
  **30-day completion rate**, and a small **14-day heatmap** so you can see momentum at
  a glance.
- Give habits a colour and an icon, and link them to a life area.
- Archive habits you've paused; restore them later.

### 5.5 Timetable

The timetable is your **ideal recurring week**.

- See a **Monday–Sunday grid**, with Saturday and Sunday shown as the **weekend**.
- **Add a time block** with a title, a start and end time, and choose **which days it
  repeats on**:
  - **Weekdays** (Mon–Fri), **Weekend** (Sat–Sun), or **Every day** with one click, or
  - **Any specific day(s)** using the individual day toggles.
- Blocks can be colour-coded, optionally linked to a **task** and a **life area**.
- Tap a block to edit or delete it; click the **+** on any day header to add a block
  pre-set to that day. Overlapping blocks are shown side by side.

### 5.6 Calendar

See everything scheduled in one place.

- Four views: **Month**, **Week**, **Day**, and **Agenda** (the next 30 days).
- **Create events** with a title, description, location, a meeting link, all-day option,
  start/end time, and optional recurrence — and link them to a life area for colour.
- **Tasks with a do-date** and **recurring task occurrences** appear alongside events as
  distinct dashed chips, so your real commitments are visible together.

### 5.7 Notes

A place to capture ideas and reference material.

- Write notes with a title and body (Markdown or plain text).
- **Pin** important notes to the top, **archive** the ones you're done with, and
  **search** instantly by title or content.
- Link notes to a **life area**, a **project**, and **tags** for easy retrieval.

### 5.8 Journal

A private daily reflection.

- One entry per day, with an optional **mood** (Great / Good / Okay / Low / Bad), a
  title, the entry body, and a **gratitude** section.
- Add **tags**, and mark entries **private** (private by default).
- Use the date navigator and recent-entries list to revisit past reflections, and jump
  back to today with one click.

### 5.9 Rewards

Motivation you can see.

- Earn **points** for completing tasks, keeping habits, and finishing goal milestones.
- The **Rewards** page shows your **total balance** and a **recent activity ledger**.
- You decide the rules: how many points each action is worth, whether high-priority
  tasks earn a bonus, and an optional **"perfect day"** bonus when you clear everything
  planned for the day.
- Completing and later **un-completing** an action correctly adjusts your points, so the
  ledger always stays honest.

### 5.10 Life Areas

Your personal categories — the backbone of the app's colour and organisation.

- Create, rename, recolour, reorder, archive, and delete life areas.
- Every life area has a colour and an icon, which are carried through tasks, goals,
  habits, notes, projects, events, and timetable blocks.
- Deleting a life area never deletes your work — related items simply become unassigned.

### 5.11 Dashboard

Your at-a-glance summary: **Today**, **Overdue**, **Upcoming**, and your **total
points** — so you always know where you stand the moment you sign in.

## 6. Settings

Organised into five tabs:

- **Profile** — your display name, timezone, and avatar.
- **Preferences** — theme (light / dark / system), 12h or 24h clock, which day the week
  starts on, default task view, and your **reward points** rules.
- **Schedule** — your working-week pattern: a Saturday rule, which weekdays you work,
  your daily start and end times, holidays, and exceptional working days, with a live
  preview of working days in the current month.
- **Notifications** — in-app notifications, optional browser notifications, a daily
  agenda, and a default reminder time.
- **Life areas** — jump straight to managing your categories.

## 7. Everyday navigation

- **Sidebar** — grouped into **Overview** (Dashboard, Today, Calendar), **Plan**
  (Timetable, Tasks, Projects, Goals, Habits), **Reflect** (Notes, Journal, Rewards),

## 9. Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to set everything up before I start?**
No. Onboarding creates sensible defaults — including seven starter life areas — so you
can begin immediately and refine things later.

**What happens to my tasks if I delete a life area?**
Nothing is lost. The related tasks, notes, and other items simply become unassigned.

**Can I repeat a task, and still skip one occurrence?**
Yes. Recurring tasks let you **skip this time** or **remove an occurrence from the
series** without deleting the schedule.

**Does the timetable tie into my tasks?**
Yes. A timetable block can optionally link to a task, and displays that task's title.

**How are reward points kept accurate if I undo something?**
Un-completing an action reverses its points in the ledger, so your balance always
reflects what you've actually completed.

**Is my journal private?**
Journal entries are private by default.

**Can I use it on my phone?**
Yes. The layout adapts, with a slide-in navigation drawer on smaller screens.
